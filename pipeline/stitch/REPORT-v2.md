# Reality Splat v2 · 85–140 s, six lenses, official viewer chase

**Do not ship this object layer or scale it to the whole drive. The road passes; the orbit does not.** This is an offline 684.03 m slice, not a whole-drive render. The delivery is `scene/stitched-opt-v2/` on the Studio. It contains the unchanged fused road, learned dense static objects, original rig and drive, and the live-v4 sky panorama. [Reproduce the run](README.md#dense-optimisation-v2).

## What the viewer will see

The four columns in each board are **default, ego-left 60°, ego-right 60°, absolute 40° elevation**; rows are v1 and v2. Every constituent was rendered at **1440×900**. Full originals are in `work/stitch/report/v2/{v1,v2}/views/<seconds>/`. The 96 frames per complete orbit per model and four slow paired movies are in that report tree. The proof reads PLYs and renders physical Gaussian colours over the real panorama, with no viewer fog, apron, HUD, car, path, transient movers or generator hiding missing geometry. Teal/white sky visible through the verge is a **hole**, not pale asphalt. Bad novel geometry is counted against the scene even if the physical lens score improves.

**Pose source.** On basebook, `cd viewer && bun tools/chase-track.ts ../scenes/road-stitched-v2 85 140 30 1440 900` produced 1,651 time-stamped default poses. The four defaults use that JSON's actual position, quaternion and vertical FOV. All orbit positions use the same pure `chaseInputs` / `placeChase` functions; the zero orbit must agree with the official track within its serialization precision. Camera-to-world converts Three's +Y up/−Z forward to the compositor's +Y down/+Z forward. Constants: 16.2 m, 16.9° elevation, 5.3° tilt, no pan, 1.45 m forward / 0.8 m up pivot, 50° vertical FOV. Time-filtered speed gives actual radii **16.52, 16.58, 16.83, 17.56 m** at the four sites. The 6 m and 19.5 m explorations are superseded. Independent native CPU and Metal RGB at four final novel poses differ by **0.40–0.53 levels** on covered pixels. Pinhole covariance uses the frustum-clamped derivative to avoid erroneously smearing off-screen grazing ellipses; physical lenses use KB4 with 33 row poses.

**Ground passes.** All 36 original ground tile files are hard links; their complete tile metadata also matches. At the actual 112 s default, across 261,823 opaque road pixels in the lower central ROI, v2 differs from the original fused mosaic by **0.108 RGB levels** (v1: 4.916). Mean Sobel gradient is **7.21** vs the mosaic’s 7.15; the 90th percentile is 16.52 vs 16.07. The Studio evidence includes a native-resolution three-way crop, `mosaic-comparison-112.jpg`. The slight increase reflects composited objects, not sharpening of the ground. Lane-marking edges retain the original contrast.

**Sky exclusion helps, but does not recover the poles.** In the fixed 350×85 sky box at (490,85)–(840,170) in the 112 s default, v2 object opacity is zero; v1 has 1.32% of pixels above 15% opacity, with a 29 px widest component. The box does not score the lamp heads themselves. Across the 24 site/lens semantic checks, opacity over photographed sky falls **14.67% → 0.38%** and object opacity over road **1.23% → 0.14%**. But non-road coverage also falls **87.0% → 79.5%** and non-road PSNR **16.72 → 16.19 dB**. At absolute 40° the cleaner skyline coexists with missing crown tops and unrooted islands. These priors prevent visibly wrong occupancy; they cannot stand in for new observations of disoccluded surfaces.

## Dense seed and trainer decision

**Custom PyTorch-MPS with the existing MetalSplat kernels, DC only.** The stock-trainer bake-off is in the [v1 report](REPORT.md#why-this-trainer). The mesh/point constraints, row clock, per-ray confidence, layered composition and sky/free-space losses are required. Higher-order SH was not used; there is no view-dependent basis to paint over missing geometry. Zero generative inputs were used, and the optional per-pixel weighted pose interface was separately exercised on a real cached image.

**Initialise every eligible exposure without independent confirmation.** Original snapshot contained 1,855 train-safe depth maps: front 632, rear 110, left/right pillar 631/314, left/right repeater 90/89. In this ordering the available pre-exclusion counts were 1,141/160/1,139/511/156/156. Missing maps, missing producer metadata, unposed products, reserved frames or producer windows reading a reserved camera frame were not used. Original KB4 rays turn optical z into range. The stitched audited inverse-range coefficients use `r / clip(a + b·min(r,40), .55,1.55)`; the outer-rim residual from livemap's measurements provides a smoothly applied, bounded rim correction. The rear outer-rim median of −58% is corrected but its loss confidence drops as much as 65%; right repeater confidence is additionally ×0.55 near / ×0.23 beyond 10 m. Calibration is not a guarantee of distant metric accuracy. RGB-only samples add exposures with no eligible depth.

Every fourth depth-lattice pixel is sampled with an alternating phase. Metric range proposals are searched against two displaced real photographs (4.2 m longitudinal front or ~1.65 m for side/rear). When colour/gradient evidence is weak, the corrected-depth birth survives. The initial 10,746,078 samples include **1,283,026 at 60–120 m and 324,222 at 120–210 m**. Five world voxel sizes grow with distance from the trajectory; optical footprints grow with range. 6,474,303 distinct object births → 4,990,132 after repeated free-space/sky witnesses → 2,787,828 packed voxels → 2,174,730 at the start of the accepted gradient fit. Isolated births may survive. Inference products upstream changed on the shared Studio after initialisation: the original selected corrected targets are cached; the colour/silhouette posterior reads them back from those pinholes and treats unsampled native pixels as unknown. No held or replacement-window data were substituted.

**Free XYZ, full orientation, scales, opacity and colour.** Eight native-row exposure bands per real pinhole; 1152×744 front, 640×416 for the others, three rear headings; sampled 320×96/64 patches. Learnable per-physical-image log gain over the existing correction is regularised and limited to ±0.045. Anti-aliased projected area, robust RGB and spatial gradients, near absolute log depth and weak centred log-depth errors far away, planar normal/thickness agreement, multilayer log-depth variance, opacity and repeated clear-sky/road evidence. No positional anchor. Swept-hull/ground centre rejection is active during training. The final conservative grid guard compares the **full three-sigma support** against maximum road/swept-body height over its horizontal enclosure. The fused ground geometry, DC, scales, rotations, opacity and time fields are literal original files. No ground gain or residual was fitted.

**Iterations and ablations.** Dense depth alone improved horizon coverage but multiplied foliage into sheets. The first fit was abandoned: on 37 real training patches PSNR fell 19.85 → 15.62 dB by 4,800 updates, with independent CPU renders agreeing. In one 100 s pillar crop, signs of the pooled-SSIM colour gradient agreed with squared RGB error only 49% of the time (robust RGB: 94%); the term overwhelmed RGB. The rerun used bounded finite RGB differences instead of this training SSIM term. Its same-crop MSE dropped from 0.0162 to 0.0030 in 16 updates. This is a gradient sanity check, not a held-out score. The 14,400-step fit moved **1,102,900 objects over 25 cm** (median/p90/p99 movement 0.257/0.670/1.285 m before cleanup), with 119,602 splits, 117,079 clones and 267,713 prunes. This is not v1's near-frozen geometry.

The real-pose ablation compared the fitted scene, the photo/silhouette-cleaned scene and a distance-weighted combination. Unfiltered near foliage stayed too thick; applying the same cleanup everywhere removed far offices. The selected model uses stronger cleanup within 25 m of the route, blends smoothly out to 65 m and retains more fitted far-field optical thickness. Projected Gaussian tips hitting eroded observed sky reduce the offending scale axis (31,536 axes on 29,985 splats in the cleanup pass). A final support guard removed 10,433 objects and tightened 106,532 more. No region was carved relative to a target chase camera. The camera ablation was measured **after** visual selection. On the same 852 reserved exposures, the photo-conditioned dense prior scored **20.04 dB / .690 SSIM**, the unfiltered fit **20.58 / .708**, and the guarded distance blend **19.28 dB**. Thinning the foliage reduced occlusion in the orbit, but removed coverage of genuine surfaces. The last 6,300 real-photo updates adjusted only DC and bounded opacity on that selected geometry. They recovered **0.44 dB** to **19.72**, without changing any position, normal, scale, rotation, visibility interval or ground tile (checked after export, maximum absolute delta zero). They did not remove the 101 s foreground patch or restore the 112 s shafts. The higher-scoring unfiltered model is not the deliverable.

## What is actually missing

Compare returned optical depth with independently tracked real-image features, using calibrated per-feature row time and displaced unreserved exposures. This is a local diagnostic, not a depth benchmark or evidence that nothing else can be recovered. Region masks, every count and the marked photo sheet are under `work/stitch/report/v2/optical-tracks.{json,jpg}`; `region-evidence.json` maps the representative **composited** depth back through nearby real cameras, so translucent foliage and occlusion make that second measure approximate.

- **93 s roofline:** 93/107 optically triangulated features with usable depth were within 15% of it; median relative disagreement 4.8%. The real frontage is partly constrained. Missing elevations behind foreground crowns are not permission to replace the whole building.
- **101 s island:** a longer 1.1/1.6 m offset on each side found no qualified three-ray canopy or kerb points in these ROIs. The short **two-distinct-exposure** check (0.55 m effective baseline) did find depth agreement within 15% on 166/181 canopy/trunk and 424/428 kerb points. Real near-side surfaces are observed. Their opposite/top views and the repeated fine branches are not similarly established; do not describe all foliage or all kerb as unobserved.
- **112 s office and utility pole:** of the usable triangulated features in their small ROIs, 1/7 office and 0/6 pole features agreed within 15%. Median discrepancies were 26% and 34%, respectively. Thin shafts can disappear under the sky mask and finite depth resolution. A plausible line between pole bases and heads is not a recovered wire or lamp.
- **128 s far office/roadside trees:** median triangulated range 143.5 m; only 7/65 corresponding depth samples agreed within 15%, median disagreement 83%. Far geometry exists but the far wall and intervening crowns are not metrically resolved. Favour view-fixer weights on this facade/skyline and disoccluded verge, not high-confidence lane paint.

## Resources and 3.3 km projection

**Measured on the shared Studio (M5 Ultra, 96 GB).** 3,223 physical training exposures became 4,021 virtual views in 22 s. Dense birth took 48 s, free-space clearance 56 s, initial photographic conditioning 62 s. The geometry fit took **1,644 s (27.4 min), 8.76 cropped bands/s**, averaging 36,608 candidate and 18,466 updated objects per band. It sampled 3,033 physical exposure IDs. Peak process RSS 6.89 GB, MPS driver 3.30 GB; unified-memory figures overlap. Final visibility conditioning took 62 s, blend and guard ~3 s, fixed-geometry colour **189 s (33.3 bands/s, 1.84 GB RSS / 1.12 GB MPS)**. These principal stages total **~35 min** for the slice, excluding upstream reconstruction, rejected trials, candidate comparisons, verification and evaluation. Final native + orbit raster took 66 s for v2 before boards, video encoding and LPIPS. The rectified targets occupy 8.3 GiB.

**Output:** 3,737,013 ground + 1,403,308 object splats = **5,140,321**, in 368 tiles. The verified PLY payload is 493,672,847 bytes; all scene files together are 495,126,435 bytes. The exported rig and drive match their originals; unit normals and quaternions, finite geometry, bounds, colours, per-tile counts and ordered static visibility intervals were checked on all tiles. Sky matches live-v4 byte-for-byte. No whole-drive scene was produced.

**Projection, inputs ready:** 35 min × (3,300 / 684.03 m) × (400 / 300 overlap factor) ≈ **3 h 45 min** on one comparable Studio; budget **3–5 h** for fitting/conditioning, before full-route evaluation, upstream depth/masks or generative repair. This assumes comparable exposure density and 11 sequential 300 m ownership cores with 50 m observation overlap on either end. Keep 210–215 m far-field projection context separate from ownership; stream halo tiles rather than adding their counts. Assign a canonical tile/core and route branch to each stable primitive, freeze neighbouring non-owned geometry in the final border pass, and deduplicate births/splits across overlaps using parent IDs or position/normal/colour/depth agreement. Merge exposure runs once, blend optical thickness and DC in a common gauge and inspect both directions at seams. This merge is a plan, not a tested whole-drive implementation. Count projection: **15,622,867 actual fused ground + 1,403,308 × 3,300 / 684.03 ≈ 22.39 M total**, roughly **2.15 GB of PLYs** at the observed 96 bytes/record. The quality failures above are why this is a cost projection, not a recommendation to run it.
